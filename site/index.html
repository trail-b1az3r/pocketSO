01 — THE IDEA

Not another live USB.
A real system that moves.

Live environments disappear when you reboot. Pocketso is designed to stay with you: a full, persistent Linux installation on a separate USB drive, using the computer's hardware without taking over its internal storage.

Your packages, files, settings, and desktop travel together. Connect the drive to a supported PC and boot into your own workspace.

04 / HARDWARE RESPECT

INTERNAL DISKS
READ-ONLY GUARD

Designed to leave disks alone.

Pocketso targets a separate USB drive. Disk Guard adds a read-only safeguard for internal physical disks; it is defense-in-depth, not a hardware write blocker.

03 — UP AND RUNNING

Three steps.
One portable PC.

You'll use one USB to start the installer and a second USB as your Pocketso system drive.

01↓

Get the image

Download the Pocketso installer ISO and write it to a USB drive with the Pocketso Flasher or your preferred imaging tool.

INSTALLER USB
02⌁

Install to USB

Boot the installer, connect to the internet, and use the graphical installer to set up a separate USB drive of at least 30 GB.

DESTINATION USB · 30 GB MIN.
03↗

Take it anywhere

Shut down, move your Pocketso drive to another supported computer, and select it in that computer's boot menu.

YOUR DESKTOP, ON THE MOVE
i

Good to know: Installation currently requires an internet connection. A 64 GB USB 3.x drive is recommended. The destination USB is erased during installation.
